       MODULE 1: AI
    RESEARCH PLANNING
     Turn a broad topic into a clear research plan


                                                                                                            OVERVIEW
Research becomes time-consuming when you start searching without knowing exactly what you need to find. AI can help you plan your
research first by turning one broad topic into focused questions, useful search keywords and information gaps.




WHAT YOU WILL LEARN
   Research Questions - Break a broad topic into clear questions you need to answer.
   Search Keywords - Turn those questions into specific terms for more focused searching.
   Information Gaps - Identify what you still need to find or verify.
   Research Structure - Organise your research before spending time searching.
