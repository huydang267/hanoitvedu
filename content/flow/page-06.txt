START WITH THE TOPIC

01

First, tell AI what you're trying to do.
Don't just give AI a topic. Give it topic + purpose, so it understands what kind of research you need. A breaking-news brief, an investigative feature,
or an in-depth analysis will need very different questions even for the same topic.

EXAMPLE

✕ INSTEAD OF:

Topic: Hanoi's new electric
bus route

+

“Tell me about Hanoi's new
electric bus.”

✓ TRY:

+ “I need to research Hanoi's new

electric bus route for a news article.”

Purpose: Preparing a news
article

A SECOND EXAMPLE: SAME TOPIC, DIFFERENT PURPOSE, DIFFERENT RESULT:
PURPOSE

TYPE OF QUESTIONS AI WILL PRIORITIZE

Breaking news

Who, what, when: core facts needed immediately

In-depth analysis Why, who benefits, long-term impact, comparison with similar cases
Interview piece

Open-ended questions to draw out perspective, contradictions to clarify with the source

Infographic

Standout figures, easy-to-visualize facts, timeline

TIP FOR EDITORIAL TEAMS
If the piece has a tight deadline, say so in the
prompt (“300-word brief, due in 1 hour”).

KEY IDEA
Clear purpose → More relevant research
