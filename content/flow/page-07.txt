Turn one broad topic

“Generate 5 key questions I should investigate

KEY IDEA

AI CAN HELP YOU IDENTIFY:
WHEN?

When will it launch?

WHERE?

Which areas will it connect?

WHO?

Who is responsible for operating and funding it?

WHAT?

What are the operating hours and frequency?

WHY?

Why is the route being introduced now?

TIP FOR EDITORIAL TEAMS

KEY IDEA
Broad topic → Focused questions

For time-sensitive news, ask AI to rank the
questions by priority (which need answering
before publishing, which can be added later).
This keeps you from burning your time budget
on secondary details.
