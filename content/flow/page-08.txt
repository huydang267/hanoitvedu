TIP FOR EDITORIAL TEAMS

KEY IDEA
Better keywords → More focused search

Try keywords in both Vietnamese and English
if the topic has an international angle (e.g.,
foreign sponsors, imported technology), and
favor keywords with proper nouns, numbers,
and specific dates. They filter out much more
accurate results than generic terms.
