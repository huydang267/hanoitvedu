ABOUT US
HaNoiTVEdu is a workplace learning platform designed to help Hanoi TV employees build
practical AI skills for their everyday work.
We focus on role-based, hands-on learning for Editorial, Admin and Content teams — helping
employees understand when to use AI, which tools fit different tasks, and how to use them
responsibly.
Through short learning modules, practical exercises, AI tool guidance and peer sharing,
HaNoiTVEdu turns AI from a new technology into a practical work skill.
